Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about scoring, pricing, and what the badge means.

How is the badge score calculated?

We check liquidity lock status, contract safety (via GoPlus and Honeypot.is), holder concentration, and community activity, currently through manual review using these public tools. See the full breakdown on our Methodology page.

What's the difference between a free check and the paid badge?

Running your project through GoPlus or Honeypot.is yourself is free and gives a moment-in-time result that isn't saved anywhere. The £29 badge gives you a dated, permanent report, a clickable badge you can embed on your own site, and a listing in our directory — a lasting reference rather than a one-off check.

Is the badge a guarantee my token is safe?

No. It's a transparent snapshot of what our checks found at review time — not a formal audit, not a guarantee of performance, and not investment advice. A Low Risk score means our checks didn't find red flags at that point; it can't predict future team decisions or market conditions.

How long does a review take?

Typically within 24 hours of confirmed payment.

What happens if my badge isn't renewed?

A one-time Basic badge moves to an "Expired" status if not renewed within 30 days, since the underlying data can change. Growth and Priority plans include ongoing monthly re-verification so the badge stays "Active."
