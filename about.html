About Cryptonextx

Who runs this, what we actually check, and what we deliberately don't claim.

Who runs this

Cryptonextx is an independently run project, currently operated as an individual venture (not yet a registered company). Research and review content is published under the byline "Mara Voss" — a consistent editorial name used for Cryptonextx's content while the project is at this early, pre-company stage. See our Methodology page for exactly how badge scoring works.

What sources we rely on

  • DEX Screener / DEX Tools — liquidity value and lock status
  • GoPlus Security — smart contract safety signals
  • Honeypot.is — buy/sell simulation to catch trading traps
  • Manual review of public Telegram/X activity for signs of genuine engagement

Reviews are currently performed manually against these sources, with AI tools used only to organize findings and draft the final report — not to generate a score without a human checking the underlying data first. See the Methodology page for the full scoring breakdown.

What we deliberately don't do

  • We don't recommend buying or selling any token
  • We don't guarantee a token's future performance
  • We don't perform a formal, licensed security audit — the badge is a lighter-weight, transparent alternative for early-stage projects
  • We don't issue a badge without checking the underlying data ourselves first

Conflicts of interest

Projects pay for a badge review. We disclose this openly: payment covers the review process and listing, not a guaranteed outcome — a project can and does receive a Medium or High Risk result if that's what the data shows. See our Methodology page for the risk bands.
