How the AI Safety Badge Is Calculated

Full transparency on what we check, how we score it, and — just as importantly — what the badge does not mean.

What we actually check

Before any project is listed or messaged, and again before a badge is issued, our AI engine and screening tools evaluate the following public, on-chain and off-chain data points:

Liquidity Lock

We verify whether liquidity is locked, the percentage locked, and the lock duration. Projects with unlocked or partially locked liquidity are flagged.

Checked on-chain

Contract Safety Scan

The smart contract is scanned for common risk patterns — hidden mint functions, ownership renouncement, honeypot mechanics, and transfer restrictions.

Via GoPlus Security & Honeypot.is

Holder Distribution

We analyze how concentrated token holdings are. A small number of wallets holding a large share of supply increases the risk score.

On-chain holder data

Community Activity

We assess whether engagement on X and Telegram reflects a real, active community rather than bot-driven or purchased activity.

Public social data

Liquidity Depth

Total liquidity value is measured against typical trading volume to flag projects vulnerable to price manipulation.

On-chain / DEX data

Project Age & History

We note how long the contract has been live and whether ownership, liquidity, or supply have changed unexpectedly since launch.

On-chain history

How the risk score works

Every reviewed project receives one of three risk bands. The band reflects what our checks actually find — it is not guaranteed to be positive, and two projects that both pass our initial screening can still receive different scores.

Low Risk Liquidity fully locked, clean contract scan, healthy holder distribution, genuine community activity.
Medium Risk Passes core checks but shows one or more caution signals — e.g. liquidity near the minimum threshold, or moderate holder concentration.
High Risk Fails one or more core checks. Projects in this band are not issued a badge until the underlying issue is resolved.

Badge status

A badge is not permanent by default — its status reflects whether the project has kept its analysis current.

Active

Re-analyzed within the last billing cycle (Growth / Priority plans). Score reflects current on-chain data.

AI-Verified

Priority-tier badge, backed by extended analysis and monthly re-verification.

Expired

No re-analysis within 30 days of a one-time Basic listing. The badge displays as unverified until renewed.

What this badge is not

  • It is not a substitute for a professional smart contract audit performed by a licensed security firm.
  • It does not guarantee the project's team is trustworthy, that funds won't be mismanaged, or that the token will perform well.
  • It cannot detect risks that aren't visible in public on-chain or social data, such as off-chain agreements or future team decisions.
  • It is not investment, financial, or legal advice, and should never be the sole basis for an investment decision.
  • A "Low Risk" score describes the data available at the time of review — it is not a permanent or forward-looking guarantee.
